What the levels mean

Spot — current price. Schwab line uses the front ES futures last (ESZ26 etc.); ZeroGEX uses the price its levels were computed against.

Gamma Flip — the strike where net dealer gamma changes sign. Above it dealers are typically long gamma (dampen moves); below it they are short gamma (amplify moves).

Call Wall — strike with the largest positive gamma (usually a call concentration). Often acts as resistance when dealers are long gamma.

Put Wall — strike with the largest negative gamma (usually a put concentration). Often acts as support or a magnet when dealers are short gamma.

What the 25Δ risk reversal shows

25Δ RR — Put IV minus call IV at approximately 25 delta for the front expiration (volatility points). Positive means puts are richer than calls (normal for equity indexes).
vs 5-day avg — How far the current reading is from the average of recent stored points. Positive = skew is elevated vs recent history (more demand for downside protection).
Regime — Where the current value ranks in the recent distribution. Elevated = top quartile, Low = bottom quartile, Normal = middle.
A rising line means puts are getting more expensive relative to calls. A falling line means downside protection is cheapening.
