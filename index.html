Rules used (simple priors — not financial advice)
  • Long: Regime LONG + spot above flip + near put wall + rising/stable 5-day net GEX
  • Short: Regime SHORT + spot below flip + near call wall + falling 5-day net GEX
  • Straddle: Spot within ~0.4% of gamma flip + large |net GEX| or unstable regime (flip zone)

Scores are weighted qualitative priors from dealer-gamma mechanics. History is read live from the shared store so desktop and mobile see the same metrics. New points are added every 15 min while the page is open (and once daily via cron).

Data sources & refresh
Levels (spot, flip, walls, regime, net GEX): ZeroGEX free delayed (ES modeled from SPX options, ~15+ min behind) · app polls every 60s
Strike profile: AlgoX Flow SPY ×10 → ES · full spread, 25-pt bars within ±500 of spot
Prices (/ES, 10Y, BZ Oil): Yahoo Finance via proxy · app polls every 60s
5-day net GEX history + indicator metrics: live from shared store · page-open posts every 15 min + daily cron · same on every device

When data is flowing vs closed
• /ES futures (Yahoo price): nearly 24/5 — Sunday ~6:00 PM ET through Friday ~5:00 PM ET, with a brief daily maintenance break (~5:00–6:00 PM ET). Outside these hours quotes may be stale or unavailable.
• SPX options / ZeroGEX levels & AlgoX SPY profile: most useful during US cash-equity hours (regular session ~9:30 AM–4:00 PM ET). Outside RTH the free delayed feeds may freeze, lag more, or return the last available snapshot. Extended hours exist but gamma modeling is thinner.
• 10Y & BZ Oil (Yahoo): follow their respective futures/cash sessions; typically available when those markets are open.
• History collection continues on its schedule regardless of market hours, storing whatever the sources return (including stale values when closed).
