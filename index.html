Rules used (simple priors — not financial advice)
  • Long: Regime LONG + spot above flip + near put wall + rising/stable net GEX
  • Short: Regime SHORT + spot below flip + near call wall + falling net GEX
  • Straddle: Spot within ~0.4% of gamma flip + large |net GEX| or unstable regime

What the 25Δ risk reversal shows

A drop means put skew is cheapening relative to calls (less demand for downside protection). A rise means puts are getting more expensive vs calls. Values are in volatility points for the front expiration.
